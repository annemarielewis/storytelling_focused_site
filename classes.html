Producing: From Pre-Production to Post

Learn the producer’s role across the entire filmmaking process—from developing and planning a project, through production on set, to post-production and delivery.

Explore how to write for nonfiction when the story is discovered rather than completely predetermined—including research, interviews, story structure, archival material, and writing with real people and events.

The Art of Finding a Story

How to Make Something Boring or Complicated Engaging

Identify your target audience and learn how to find the human story, dramatic question, emotional hook, and compelling point of view hiding inside subjects that initially seem ordinary, technical, overwhelming, or “boring.” This class can apply to documentaries, branded content, journalism, corporate films, and narrative work.
